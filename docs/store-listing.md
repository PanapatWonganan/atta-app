## English (en-US)

**APP NAME (27/30)**

ATTA: Manifest Affirmations

**SHORT DESCRIPTION (77/80)**

Daily affirmations for women, read aloud. Manifest, widget, gentle reminders.

**FULL DESCRIPTION**

Your words shape your day.

ATTA gives you one affirmation each morning, written to be spoken out loud, and a quiet way to actually practice it. No noisy feed. Just your line, read to you in a calm voice.

WHY THIS ONE
Most affirmation apps hand you a quote and a paywall. ATTA is built around practice: it reads each line aloud, over rain or waves if you like, so you can close your eyes and repeat it. There's a sleep timer for drifting off to your affirmations at night.

DAILY AFFIRMATIONS, WRITTEN FOR WOMEN
Short lines about self-worth, love, money, and calm. Real Thai and real English, not machine translation. Pick the areas that fit your life and the feed stays personal.

MANIFEST WHAT YOU'RE WORKING TOWARD
A dedicated Manifest collection helps you phrase what you want as if it's already yours. You can write your own lines too. They stay on your phone; we never read or sell what you write.

THE SMALL THINGS
• A home screen widget with the day's line
• Reminders at the times you choose: morning, midday, before bed
• A one-tap mood check-in to notice how you're doing
• Share cards that actually look good on a story

FREE AND PREMIUM, HONESTLY
The free version is genuinely usable: daily affirmations plus the widget and reminders. You can also watch a short ad for a full day pass to everything. Premium removes ads and opens every category, voice, and sound.

Start with one line tomorrow morning. That's kind of the whole idea.

## Why these choices

- **App name** leads with the brand then spends the remaining characters on
  the two ranking keywords: "Manifest" (the differentiator ATTA's content
  is centered on, lower competition than "affirmations" alone) and
  "Affirmations" / "คำยืนยันรายวัน" (the head keyword the category leader
  also ranks on). No slogan words.
- **Short description** contains the head keyword + audience ("daily
  affirmations for women") and the two most tangible features (read aloud,
  widget/reminders). It doubles as the above-the-fold hook.
- **Competitor complaints used**: "I am" users complain about too little
  free content and an aggressive paywall → the "FREE AND PREMIUM, HONESTLY"
  section and the "quote and a paywall" line answer that directly, without
  naming the app.
- **Keyword density**: "affirmation(s)" ×5 in EN full text, "Manifest" ×3;
  "คำยืนยัน" ×5 in TH. Don't add more — it starts reading as stuffing.
- **Policy-safe**: no competitor names, no "#1/best" claims, no medical
  promises (nothing about anxiety/depression), zero exclamation marks.
- If you edit the name or short description, re-count characters (Thai
  combining marks each count as one character on Play).
