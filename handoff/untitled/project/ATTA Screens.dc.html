Why this closes
The paywall arrives after the result screen has shown a real personalized line, so it asks the user to keep something they already have — a stronger position than promising something unseen.
The trial timeline defuses the number-one cancellation anxiety ("I'll forget and get charged") by making the Day-5 reminder a stated feature. Apps that adopted this pattern report higher trial starts than urgency timers — and it is the only version consistent with this brand.
Yearly is pre-selected and framed per-month; Lifetime anchors it from above, Weekly from below. No strikethrough prices, no countdown, no shaking buttons.
Decline path
"Not now" keeps the free tier: one line a day, Linen theme only, no widget. The widget is the upgrade trigger — it reappears as a quiet inline card in the gallery, never as a popup.
